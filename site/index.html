Questions.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Claude remembers what you tell it. Myynd learns from what you do. It reads every project across mail, files, chats and calendar, works before you ask, and hands you the work done. And it runs on any AI, including one on your own Mac.

Does it send email for me?

Never on its own. It writes the reply and attaches the file. You read it and press send.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your Mac. Only what a task needs goes to the AI model, and nothing leaves at all if you run a local model. A page in the app lists exactly what leaves your computer.

What do I need?

A Mac, Apple silicon or Intel. Connect your sources, answer a short conversation about how you work, and your first page is ready.

Windows? iPhone?

Not yet. Mac first, done properly.

Can I cancel?

Any time. Founding members keep the $19 price for as long as they stay.

What happens in the six weeks?

Week one, we connect your sources and interview the founder. Then we set up the founder and up to five key people, build three to five automations for your real work, and check in every week against one number agreed with the CEO.

Where does our data live?

On your server, or on ours. With a local AI model, nothing leaves your building. Sign-in is limited to your company email and credentials are encrypted.

Does it act on its own?

No. It drafts, prepares and answers. A person presses the button, every time.

We run on Microsoft 365.

Outlook, OneDrive and SharePoint are written and waiting on Microsoft's verification. Tell us on the call and we plan the pilot around it.

What does it cost after the pilot?

$49 a person a month, billed yearly, from ten people. The pilot is the setup: nothing more to pay to start.

Is the shared company brain ready?

We build it with our first pilots, from January 2027. A pilot starts at the top: the founder's twin and up to five key people, each with their own, on your server or ours.

Why only three pilots?

Because we set each one up ourselves, properly. Three at once is what we can do well.
