# Myynd website: brief

**Partly self-authored.** Tobia answered the brief and four questions on
24 September 2026. The rest of the skill's interview (energy curve, the one
moment, range, one world or scenes) was not asked again: he had already said
what the page must be, and asked for it to be built. Those answers are written
below in the brand's voice and marked *(self-authored)*.

## His words

> Now we need to make the website go, a website that converts and can bring us,
> by the end of the year (today is September 24), 500 paying clients.
> The website has to be:
> - futuristic
> - modern
> - interactive
> - sell-convert-value-driven
> - aesthetic
> It has to resemble the Y Combinator outcasts' kind of ideal. This shit is
> frontier, and it has to be sold as frontier tech.

Who it sells to:

> Both. It cannot be weaker, so there should be a little pill at the end of the
> website, at the bottom, that hovers over all content. You click an individual
> company, so you get to decide. Based on which one you click, you can switch at
> any moment. It tailors the website to you. It's a different kind of website.

Price: from the business plan the other session wrote
(claude.ai/artifact/Djbw1i7ZkHxSd7MXppjk7t). Main button: **pay, then
download**. Hosting: **local first, deploy later**.

## The eight interview answers

1. **Vibe:** futuristic, modern, interactive, value-driven, aesthetic. Frontier
   tech sold as frontier tech, by outsiders. References *(self-authored)*: the
   brand's own first run (ink stage, copper light), a lab notebook, a cockpit at
   night.
2. **Journey** *(self-authored, from the plan's L9: "the promise in one
   sentence, a 60-second video of a real morning, the price, the download, and
   where the data goes")*: the promise, the morning as it is, a real morning
   done by Myynd, what it reads and what it never does, what it is worth to
   you, the price, the last doubts, the one action.
3. **Energy curve** *(self-authored)*: loud open, quiet and dark before the
   demo, loudest at the demo, calm and factual after, one last push at the end.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see the curve and the peak below.
5. **What no other site does:** his answer. The page asks who you are and
   rewrites itself for you, at any moment.
6. **Range** *(self-authored)*: dark and cinematic, but not premium-minimal
   costume. Dense where the product is (the live window, the source list,
   the numbers), bare where the claim is.
7. **One world or scenes** *(self-authored)*: two worlds, one identity, the
   brand's own rule. The **stage** (ink, copper light) is where Myynd speaks.
   The **desk** (ivory) is where the product works. The page hard-cuts between
   them like the cover and the pages of one book.
8. **Assets:** none generated. The product is the asset: the brand mark, the
   light field of the first run, the working-row flame, and live markup of the
   app's own surfaces running on labelled sample data.

## Grammar

**Stage and desk** (a named new grammar, closest to Live surface). Claims live
on the ink stage in the serif; the product lives on the ivory desk as operable
surfaces. Forbids: footage, generated imagery, gradient drift between grounds
(cuts only), fake screenshots (every surface computes its state from data in
the page and says it is a sample), invented statistics.

Why the other seven lost: filmic one-shot needs footage and hides seams this
brand wants to show; chaptered editorial is too quiet for "frontier"; live
surface bans the claim a paying stranger needs in the first second; continuous
world has no geography to travel; typographic poster wastes the one asset we
have (a product that runs); gallery sells options, not one belief; split stage
fights the audience switch (the pill already splits the page); rhythmic
cutlist cannot hold the demo long enough to be believed.

## Signature move

**The audience pill.** A pill fixed at the bottom of the screen: *For
individuals · For companies*. Press the other side and a copper scan line rises
from the pill to the top of the screen; every line it passes decodes into its
other version. Headline, demo, sources, rules, numbers, price, questions and the
light field all change together. It is the product's thesis in one gesture:
software that knows who it is talking to.

## Feeling curve

```
1  Intrigue      a claim that sounds too good, and a light that notices the pointer
2  Recognition   their own morning (or their company's queue) in small, dark lines
3  Relief, awe   PEAK. The ground cuts to ivory. A live window reads, composes, writes. They press Send
4  Trust         what it reads and what it never does, set as plain facts
5  Conviction    their own numbers, computed from two sliders
6  Desire        the founding price, and why it ends
7  Reassurance   the last doubts, answered short
8  Resolve       the light comes back, one line, one button, and it holds
```

Authored silence: act 2 is deliberately quiet and dark (small type, no motion
beyond entry) so the peak has something to arrive from.

## The peak

> "it read a whole morning in front of me, wrote the reply to the client with
> the right file attached, and I pressed send"

It lives in act 3, the longest pinned span on the page.

## Tell-someone

It's the site where **you tell it who you are and it rewrites itself for you,
then reads a morning in front of you and hands you the reply to send.**

## Journey beats

```
1  Promise       the claim, the price, the button
2  Recognition   the morning as it is
3  Turn         a morning done by Myynd (the peak)
4  Substance     sources, and the four things it never does
5  Worth        the value, in their own numbers
6  Commitment    the founding price / the pilot
7  Doubts        FAQ
8  Action        one button
```

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| Promise | flow + greet, shader light field under pointer | the brand's own first-run light: the first thing a customer will see in the app |
| Recognition | flow, lines entering on scroll | quiet before the peak; a pin here would be two pins in a row |
| Turn (peak) | reveal (hard cut to ivory) + pin, 4vh, live surface driven by progress, cues for copy | the longest hold on the page; the product runs under the hand |
| Substance | flow, two columns of labels | information, not experience: short and fast |
| Worth | pointer: range inputs, numbers computed live | the one moment the visitor types their own truth |
| Commitment | flow, price plates | plain |
| Doubts | flow, native details | compressed on purpose |
| Action | pin, short, light field returns, CTA holds | the page ends somewhere |
